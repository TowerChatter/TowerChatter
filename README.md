TowerChatter is a free Windows app for the Uniden BC125AT scanner. It connects to the scanner over USB, shows the frequency and channel it is receiving, and mirrors the scanner’s display with an on-screen keypad. It captures the scanner’s audio and records MP4 video (H.264/AAC) with a live overlay of the frequency and channel, so a recording shows what you were listening to and when.

Every recording gets a frequency log (CSV and JSON) and a caption file (.srt) beside it, which makes the footage easy to review and edit in programs like Premiere Pro. The dashboard is a board of widgets you can move and resize, including recent activity, audio level, recent recordings and nearby aircraft. It has a mini window, system tray control and hotkeys.

TowerChatter is made for aviation and other radio monitoring. It checks for updates and shows a pop-up, but nothing downloads or installs without your click. It records with FFmpeg, which it downloads for you on first run.
